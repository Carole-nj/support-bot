# Acme Store Knowledge Base (replace with your own content)

## Shipping
- Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days.
- Free standard shipping on orders over $50.
- You receive a tracking link by email once your order ships.

## Returns and refunds
- You can return items within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.
- Start a return at acme.example/returns using your order number.

## Payments
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and mobile money.
- Your card is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed.

## Account
- Reset your password using "Forgot password" on the login page.
- To change your delivery address before shipping, contact support within 2 hours of ordering.

## Contact
- Email: support@acme.example
- Hours: Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
